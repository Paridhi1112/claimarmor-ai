SECTION 5: PARTS & REPAIR STANDARDS
=========================================================
OEM Parts Clause (Endorsement P-7):
  For covered vehicles less than three (3) model years old at the time of loss,
  the insurer SHALL authorize and pay for Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
  replacement parts. This applies to all structural and cosmetic components
  required to restore the vehicle to its pre-loss condition.

  For vehicles three (3) or more model years old, the insurer may authorize the
  use of Like-Kind-Quality (LKQ), aftermarket, or reconditioned parts provided
  they meet or exceed OEM specifications. The insured may request OEM parts, but
  the insurer's financial obligation will not exceed the cost of LKQ equivalent.

Labor Rate Clause:
  All repair labor rates shall be based on the prevailing market labor rate for
  certified collision repair facilities within a 25-mile radius of the insured's
  listed garaging address. The insurer may not apply a rate lower than the average
  published rate for the region as determined by third-party market surveys.

Paint & Materials:
  Paint and materials reimbursement shall be calculated using the most current
  MOTOR or Mitchell International database rates for the applicable repair region.

=========================================================
SECTION 6: TOTAL LOSS PROVISIONS
=========================================================
A vehicle shall be declared a total loss when the cost of repair exceeds 75% of
the Actual Cash Value (ACV) of the vehicle at the time of loss. ACV is determined
using NADA, Kelley Blue Book (KBB), or comparable market valuation tools,
adjusted for mileage, condition, and regional market factors.

=========================================================
SECTION 7: DIMINISHED VALUE
=========================================================
This policy does NOT include first-party diminished value coverage. However, the
insured retains the right to pursue a third-party diminished value claim against
the at-fault party's insurer. In Texas, the industry-standard 17c formula
(Georgia v. State Farm) may be cited as a reference calculation methodology,
though it is not contractually binding on this policy.

=========================================================
SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS
=========================================================
This policy does not cover:
  - Mechanical breakdown or wear and tear
  - Damage from natural wear (rust, deterioration)
  - Loss due to intentional acts by the insured
  - Racing or track events
  - Commercial use (rideshare excluded unless endorsement R-2 is active)
  - Custom equipment not declared at policy inception

=========================================================
SECTION 9: CLAIMS PROCESS
=========================================================
1. Report the claim within 24 hours of the loss.
2. The insurer will assign an adjuster within 1 business day.
3. An inspection will be completed within 3 business days.
4. The insurer must provide a written estimate within 5 business days of inspection.
5. If the insured disputes the estimate, they may invoke the Appraisal Clause
   (Section 10) within 30 days of receiving the written estimate.

=========================================================
SECTION 10: APPRAISAL CLAUSE
=========================================================
If the insurer and insured disagree on the amount of loss, either party may demand
an appraisal. Each party will select a competent, disinterested appraiser. The two
appraisers will select an umpire. Agreement of any two of the three will determine
the amount of loss. Each party bears the cost of their own appraiser; umpire costs
are split equally.
